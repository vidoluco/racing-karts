Racing Karts music, for judging by ear

These were checked by measurement only: nobody has listened to them yet. Bali is the reference the others are aiming at.

  • bali

    The reference, unchanged: a Balinese kebyar, interlocking kotekan over a gong cycle.

    40 s

  • antelao

    A Ländler from a valley band: button box with helikon basses, a clarinet in thirds, a guitar on two and three, a yodel leap in the tune.

    40 s

  • pelmo

    A Blasmusik polka in the snow: Flügelhorns in thirds, Tenorhorn answering, afterbeat horns, tuba, bass drum and cymbal, sleigh bells.

    40 s

  • cortina

    A mazurka, the brightest of the Alpine three: clarinet and trumpet over a Hackbrett, the weight of each bar on its second beat.

    40 s

  • transfagarasan

    A sârbă from a taraf of lăutari: running fiddle with grace notes and slides, a nai that takes over, țambal, the cobza chopping the offbeats, a bass fiddle.

    40 s

  • bran

    A Transylvanian string band at night: a raw primás fiddle in a minor tune, a viola sawing major chords on the eighths, a bowed bass, a struck gardon in the fast turn.

    40 s

  • bucarest

    A geampara in 7/8 (2+2+3) from the old town: wet accordion, clarinet with bends, țambal, in the Hijaz mode. It should limp on purpose.

    40 s

  • bromo

    A Javanese gamelan, heavier and slower than Bali, no beating between pairs: sarons, bonangs in imbal, kendhang, a big gong, then a rebab, then the hammered sampak.

    40 s

  • jakarta

    A dangdut with a Betawi band sitting in: the gendang dut and dang, suling and tehyan, mandolin tremolo, gambang and kromong, and the horns of a traffic jam.

    40 s

  • menu

    The lobby, same tune as before on real instruments: button box, then țambal, then gamelan bells, over a village band.

    40 s

  • results

    The short flourish at the end of a race.

    11 s

  • podium

    The podium fanfare: button box, tuba, țambal roll, a gong to close.

    21 s
