# Indonesia reference (Bromo, Bali, Java karts)

For track and kart artists. Researched 04/11/2026. Coordinates are WGS84 (lat, lon) from OpenStreetMap unless noted. "DEM" means measured by me on Copernicus GLO-30. UNVERIFIED means I could not confirm it; treat it as artistic licence.

Hard rules that apply here. Temples are scenery, treated nicely: no crashing, no explosions, keep items and hazards off temple grounds, no driving through a gate into a courtyard. Offerings are never items or jokes. Bromo and Semeru are sacred to the Tengger, so do not use Kasada offerings as a game element. Natural disasters follow the spec section of that name: cartoon physics, no casualties shown, no named death tolls. Keep ash, lava bombs and the lahar off Pura Luhur Poten and every other temple, with "never hit by explosions" applied to all of them. Outside the set pieces the normal state is one soft white wisp on Semeru and the steam column on Bromo. This page does not research disasters.

## Corrections to the spec (spec tables already updated)

| Spec said | Reality |
|---|---|
| Semeru puffing on the horizon from the sand sea | Hidden by the south caldera rim from most of the floor, including Pura Luhur Poten. Visible from the east of the sand sea and from the north rim. See viewpoints |
| Pura Ulun Danu Beratan "tiered meru" | On the lake: an 11-tier meru on one islet and a 3-tier meru on the other. A 7-tier stands on land nearby |
| Gunung Agung 3031 m | Disputed. 3142 m (Global Volcanism Program) versus about 3031 m (IFSAR and climbers, OSM). DEM top 3014. Keep 3031 |
| Jukung "striped sail" | A crab-claw sail: long triangle on two spars. Plain red or orange, or big colour blocks in red, yellow and green. Stripes are licence |
| Jeep "faded" | Real ones run from faded to bright, red, green. Faded is fine. Local name "Hardtop"; the model is a brand, keep ours generic |
| Komodo dragon in Indonesia's group | True to Indonesia, not to Java or Bali: wild only on Komodo, Rinca, Flores, Gili Dasami and Gili Motang ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komodo_dragon)). Fine as a character, never as local wildlife in scenery |
| Becak "the driver" | The driver sits behind the passenger, and the two wheels are at the front. See karts |

## Music (`indonesia`)

No standard pitch exists: each gamelan is tuned to itself, and octaves are stretched to about 1210 cents.

| Scale | Cents above the first note | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pelog, 7 tones | 0, 128, 267, 563, 702, 814, 979, octave 1214 | One published set, a starting point, every gamelan differs. Steps: 128, 139, 296, 139, 112, 165, 235 | [blog](https://preinfarction.blogspot.com/2025/03/gamelon-scales-pelog-and-slendro.html), [Pelog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelog) |
| Pelog selisir, Bali | 0, 128, 267, 702, 814 (derived by me from the row above, picking degrees 1 2 3 5 6) | Most common Bali mode, used by gong kebyar. Sources number the degrees differently: Pelog page says 1 3 5 6 7, kebyar page 1 2 3 5 6. Note names ding, dong, deng, dung, dang. Pattern is small, small, big, small, big | [Gong kebyar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamelan_gong_kebyar), Pelog |
| Pelog nem or lima, Java | 1 2 3 5 6 of the pelog row | Central Java, I found nothing Tengger-specific | Pelog |
| Slendro, 5 tones | About 240 equal steps (0, 240, 480, 720, 960, 1210); real sets range 206 to 269 | Use for `bromo`. Avoid on `bali`: in Bali, slendro is the tuning of gamelan angklung, played at cremations, and feels sad | [Slendro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slendro), blog above |
