# Cadore reference (Borca di Cadore, Boite valley, Pelmo)

For track and kart artists. Researched 04/11/2026. Coordinates are WGS84 (lat, lon) from OpenStreetMap unless noted. "DEM" means measured by me on Copernicus GLO-30. UNVERIFIED means I could not confirm it; treat it as artistic licence.

Hard rules that apply here: the churches are scenery, treated nicely, no explosions, no driving through. Natural disasters follow the spec section of that name: cartoon physics, no casualties shown, no named death tolls, no real victims or dates in the game. This page does not research them.

## Corrections to the spec (spec table already updated)

| Spec said | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Caregon del Doge" listed as a separate landmark | It is a nickname of Monte Pelmo itself, for the seat shape of its cirque. Also "el Caregón de 'l Pareterno". Not a separate object |
| Pelmetto footprints near Passo Staulanza | Real, but on foot only: 1.7 km from the pass at bearing 129, 2050 m, CAI trail 472, about 1 h 45 up. Not at the road. A full-scale cast is in the museum at Selva di Cadore |
| Church "by Edoardo Gellner" | Gellner with Carlo Scarpa (who joined in September 1956), built from 1955 to about 1960, consecrated 1961, a true A-frame |
| `pelmo` horizon from Staulanza | Antelao is hidden behind Pelmo from the pass. See viewpoints |
