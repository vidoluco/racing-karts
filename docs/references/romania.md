# Romania reference (Transfăgărășan, Bran, Maramureș karts)

For track and kart artists. Researched 04/11/2026. Coordinates are WGS84 (lat, lon) from OpenStreetMap unless noted. "DEM" means measured by me on Copernicus GLO-30. UNVERIFIED means I could not confirm it; treat it as artistic licence.

Hard rules that apply here. Treat Bran as a friendly place, no gore, no horror, no impaling jokes. Natural disasters follow the spec section of that name: cartoon physics, no casualties shown, no named death tolls. Bâlea has a real deadly avalanche in its history (see the Bâlea Lake Wikipedia page): the spec's snow slide stays a cartoon, with no reference to that event, its date or its victims. This page does not research disasters. Bears: see the note below.

## Corrections to the spec (spec tables already updated)

| Spec said | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Capra tunnel" | Official name Tunelul Capra-Bâlea. 884 m (en.wikipedia) or 887 m (ro.wikipedia, OSM tag). One web page says 5 km: wrong |
| Moldoveanu 2544 m on the Bâlea horizon | Not visible from Bâlea Lac nor from anywhere on the road near it: the Capra and Vânătoarea lui Buteanu wall hides it. Visible from the ridge east of Valea Bâlii, see viewpoints. The track stands at the lake and leaves it out, see `transfagarasan.md` |
| Mocănița "green and red" | The loco is black with a red frame and red wheels. The carriages are green. Keep it black and red |
| Sphinx and Babele as Bran scenery | They are on the Bucegi plateau 14 km SE of Bran, hidden behind the western Bucegi wall. Set piece only, never horizon |
| "Saxon fortified village houses" at Bran | Saxons built the castle, but the fortified villages (Hărman, Prejmer) are 33 to 39 km NE, past Brașov. Bran's own villages are Romanian mountain houses. Fine as a cartoon mix, not a local fact |
| Maramureș gate at Bran | Maramureș is 250 km north. Licence, keep it as a gift from the Mocănița region |

## Bears

Roadside bears are real but on the south side, on Arefu to Vidraru, not at Bâlea. Feeding is fined and is a real public safety problem, with bears relocated to the Libearty sanctuary at Zărnești. Make Ursu and the hazard bear a harmless passer-by: never show anyone feeding him, never a person in danger, never attack gags. Spec row adjusted. Source: [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/romanian-authorities-begin-relocation-bears-transfagarasan-2024), [fines](https://romania-insider.com/two-fines-travelers-feed-bears-romanias-transfagarasan-2024).
