# Backdrops: credits

The Antelao track's panorama is one continuous 360 degree painting, cut into tiles (`def.backdrops`, `docs/backdrops-howto.md`). Every tile comes from `tools/compose-panorama.py` with `tools/panoramas/antelao.json`. The massif left of the Antelao is the Sorapiss side as that photo shows it, brought down in height by the recipe's `squash`. Everything else is procedural painted slopes.

## Terrazze di Bali

One panorama, `tools/panoramas/bali.json`, painted by `tools/compose-panorama.py` with the `tropical` preset brought to the repaint's colours (`preset_over`). Viewpoint -8.41, 115.45, above Muncan on the Rendang road. Gunung Agung is a hand repaint of one real photo, kept as it is (`painted: true`), at its true bearing, its whole cone scaled from 13.9 to 19 degrees. The photo's clouds hide the top and the lower flanks, so the summit line and the wide lower flanks are inferred in the painting. On 06/11 that painting was regraded by `tools/panoramas/bali-agung-grade.py` (`agung-graded.jpg`) to what the Muncan and Bandem photos show: hazy blue brown cone, fine gullies, forest belt on the lower flanks; its outline did not change. The rest of the circle is the real skyline from the DEM in the same air, with the sea at sea level and Nusa Penida and the south lifted to clear the near ground (`dem_lift`).

## Jakarta

Two volcanoes, ridges traced from the photos, keep their true bearings and are drawn larger than life. The earlier hand repaint is no longer used.
